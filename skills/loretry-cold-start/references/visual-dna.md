# A circle draws a sine wave

Sage paper · Shared baseline · Horizontal projection · Continuous trace

A horizontal guide carries the point’s height from the unit circle to the plot. Sage paper and quiet axes let the growing copper trace lead the eye.

## Visual design specifications

- Composition: Keep the explanation on the left and the diagram on the right. Align the unit circle’s center with the plot’s zero line at y = 196 within the SVG.
- Color and line: Use sage for the paper, dark green for type and the radius, and copper for projected height and the wave. A dashed guide distinguishes the construction from the solid result.
- Motion: Copy the circle point’s height directly across while the angle advances to the right. Keep previous heights as a continuous trace and complete one turn at 14.2 seconds.
- Static view: Use the completed wave as the poster. Keep 0, 2π, and ±1 legible and let the viewer start the animation when ready.
