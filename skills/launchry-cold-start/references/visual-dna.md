# A task moves into the day

Warm paper · Fixed workspace · Gold task card · Visible confirmation

The Dayfold workspace stays fixed while one gold task moves from the inbox into the calendar. A six-second hold gives the viewer time to check the result.

## Visual design specifications

- Composition: Place the copy in the left third of the 1280 × 720 frame. Keep the 720 px interface on the right with the inbox and calendar visible together.
- Color: Use warm paper behind the interface and cobalt for text and controls. Reserve gold for the selected task; switch it to pale blue after it lands.
- Motion: Let the pointer select the task and activate Schedule for today. Move just that card from 6.9 to 8.6 seconds. The rest of the interface stays still.
- Static view: Hold on the result from 10 seconds. Keep the moved task and the 09:30–10:30 confirmation in view. Use this frame as the poster and keep playback user-initiated.

## Palette

- `#F7F0DF` Warm paper behind the workspace
- `#082590` Cobalt headings and controls
- `#F5C15C` Gold for the selected task
- `#E1E8FF` Pale blue for the scheduled card

7.8 s · The task straddles the two panes. The surrounding interface stays still, so the movement has a visible origin and destination.

12 s · The final state shows the task, its assigned time, and the two untouched tasks.
